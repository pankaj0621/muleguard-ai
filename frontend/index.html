1. Executive summary

Between 21 and 31 March 2025, a set of eleven bank accounts exhibited coordinated fund-movement behaviour inconsistent with the declared customer profiles. Funds credited to the primary account were distributed onward within a median retention interval of nineteen minutes. Six independently filed fraud complaints reference accounts within this set. The analysis presented here is an investigative signal derived from transactional patterns and complaint linkage; it does not constitute legal determination of criminal conduct.
